# Security

HARMONY runs on your own PC, can read your screen, hear you and act on your computer, so security problems matter.

**Reporting:** please report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub's "Report a vulnerability" (Security tab) rather
than in a public issue. Include what an attacker could do and how to reproduce it. You'll get a reply as soon as
possible; there is no bug bounty.

**How HARMONY is defended** is described in `docs/SECURITY.md` and `docs/HOW-IT-WORKS.md`: outside text is data, never
instructions; risky actions need the owner; workers run fixed scripts; models are pinned by hash; secrets are masked
before anything leaves the PC; and monthly drills attack her on purpose.
